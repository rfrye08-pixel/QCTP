## 3. Current gaps against the hardened standard

### D1-GAP-01 — Breathing is underspecified

Current language says to breathe more slowly but does not specify route, cadence, volume, no-hold rule, or transition to natural breathing.

### D1-GAP-02 — State markers are not presented before practice

The user is not told that success means lower unnecessary effort, emotional-state transfer, attention recovery, broader awareness, and continuous alertness—not unusual sensations.

### D1-GAP-03 — Timer completion and capability are not separated

A full run can mark morning completion even if audio fails or the user sleeps through most of the session.

### D1-GAP-04 — No explicit drowsiness or over-effort correction

The session needs a concise correction for sleep drift, ocular strain, breath discomfort, and rigid concentration.

### D1-GAP-05 — Released audio is not local-pack controlled

The current candidate references external neural-audio URLs and has already produced a physical-iPhone silent-audio regression.

### D1-GAP-06 — No state-gated post-session score

The workbook is useful but does not produce Introduced/Accessed capability status from state-specific markers.

### D1-GAP-07 — Post-session capture is not voice-first

The user should be able to speak the raw experience immediately.

### D1-GAP-08 — Face-down instruction is not fully accepted

The first complete hardened release shall use screen-up low-brightness unless physical acceptance explicitly validates face-down/background continuation.

## 5. Pre-session orientation

This teaching occurs before the timed meditation and is not part of the 25 minutes.

### Target capabilities

1. reduce unnecessary physical effort without becoming sleepy;
2. generate appreciation and retain the state after releasing the memory;
3. recognize attention capture and return without frustration;
4. shift from one-object attention to broad open monitoring;
5. remain aware during brief content-light intervals.

### What not to chase

- vibrations;
- visions;
- body disappearance;
- mystical certainty;
- completely stopping thought;
- perfect relaxation.

### Safety / setup

- seated upright;
- feet supported;
- hands resting;
- headphones tested;
- screen-up at low brightness for candidate acceptance;
- safe stationary environment;
- no driving, machinery, ladder, water, or hazardous task.

### Audio gate

The session may begin only after:

- local pack checksum pass;
- opening cue playback authorization;
- media output confirmation;
- timer and storage readiness.

## 6. Candidate timed script

Cue timing is absolute. Spoken duration may not move phase boundaries.

### Cue D1-H-000 — 0:00 — Required — Physical Settling

> Sit upright with both feet supported and your hands resting. Close your eyes. Let the spine remain neutral rather than rigid. Separate your teeth slightly and let the tongue rest. For now, do not change the breath. Feel the chair beneath you and notice the body state you brought into the session.

**Function:** orientation and baseline.  
**Silence after cue:** approximately 20 seconds.

### Cue D1-H-040 — 0:40 — Required — Physical Settling

> Move attention through the forehead, eyes, jaw, throat, shoulders, chest, abdomen, hands, pelvis, legs, and feet. At each area, feel the effort that is already present. On the next exhale, reduce that effort by about ten percent. Do not demand complete relaxation.

**Function:** operationalized release.  
**Correction:** if posture collapses, restore upright support without tensing.

### Cue D1-H-100 — 1:40 — Coach — Physical Settling

> Let breathing remain natural. Notice contact, weight, temperature, and muscle effort. If thought carries you away, return to one physical contact point, then continue the whole-body awareness.

### Cue D1-H-155 — 2:35 — Required transition

> In a moment, you will use a quiet paced breath. Slow does not mean large. Keep every breath comfortable and below a full lung inflation.

### Cue D1-H-180 — 3:00 — Required — Coherence

> Inhale quietly through the nose for four seconds. Let the lower ribs and abdomen expand gently without lifting the shoulders. Exhale smoothly for six seconds through the nose. Do not squeeze empty and do not hold. Continue at this quiet volume.

**Function:** QCTP-B1 regulated entry.  
**Failure rule:** dizziness, tingling, or air hunger pauses the practice and offers natural breathing or 5-in/5-out.

### Cue D1-H-240 — 4:00 — Teach — Coherence

> Place attention at the center of the chest. Continue the four-second inhale and six-second exhale. Imagine the breath moving through the chest area while your lungs continue breathing normally.

### Cue D1-H-300 — 5:00 — Required — Coherence

> Recall one specific moment that produces genuine appreciation, care, gratitude, or quiet awe. Use only one scene. Notice the physical response in the chest, face, breathing, or eyes.

### Cue D1-H-375 — 6:15 — Required — Coherence

> Release the scene now. Keep attention on the body state it produced. If the feeling fades, bring the scene back for one breath, then release the story again.

### Cue D1-H-435 — 7:15 — Required transition

> Stop counting the breath. Let breathing regulate itself. Keep the appreciation state without repeating words or replaying the scene.

**State check:** emotional/body shift plus retention without story.

### Cue D1-H-480 — 8:00 — Required — Focused Attention

> Release the emotional exercise. Place attention on the physical sensation of breathing at the nostrils. Do not change the breath. Count one complete inhale and exhale as one. Continue to ten, then return to one.

### Cue D1-H-555 — 9:15 — Coach — Focused Attention

> When you recognize that attention has entered a thought, sound, or body sensation, label it once as captured. Return to the nostril sensation. Restart at one only if the count was lost. Recognition and return are the training repetition.

### Cue D1-H-645 — 10:45 — Coach — Focused Attention

> Reduce effort in the eyes and forehead. The object remains the breath sensation, but concentration should not feel rigid.

### Cue D1-H-720 — 12:00 — Required — Focused Attention

> Stop counting. Follow the beginning, middle, and end of each breath sensation. Notice whether a thought can begin without automatically becoming a full storyline.

### Cue D1-H-765 — 12:45 — Required transition

> In a moment, attention will widen. You are not abandoning clarity. You are including more without selecting one thing.

### Cue D1-H-780 — 13:00 — Required — Open Monitoring

> Become aware of the three-dimensional volume occupied by the head: behind the forehead, behind the eyes, between the ears, and above the mouth. Do not visualize anatomy. Notice volume.

### Cue D1-H-855 — 14:15 — Coach — Open Monitoring

> Include the space immediately surrounding the head. If you are forcing the visual field to become dark, relax the eyes and include sound.

### Cue D1-H-945 — 15:45 — Required — Open Monitoring

> Include the chest, abdomen, pelvis, arms, hands, legs, and feet at the same time. Stop scanning one part after another. Feel the body as one volume.

### Cue D1-H-1050 — 17:30 — Required — Open Monitoring

> Expand awareness several inches around the body, then to the space between you and the room boundaries. Do not create a glowing shell. Notice the space that is already present.

### Cue D1-H-1170 — 19:30 — Coach — Open Monitoring

> Include the entire room as one volume. Let sounds occur at their locations. Avoid naming objects unless a practical safety need appears.

### Cue D1-H-1290 — 21:30 — Required — Open Monitoring

> Let breath, body sensations, sounds, visual darkness, emotion, and thoughts exist in one field. A thought may appear without being followed. If you become absorbed, return to one breath for three cycles and widen again.

### Cue D1-H-1360 — 22:40 — Required transition

> Release every active technique. No counting, breath control, scanning, or deliberate expansion. Awareness remains while events come and go.

### Cue D1-H-1380 — 23:00 — Required — Pure Observation

> Notice thoughts, sounds, sensations, and breathing without selecting one. Do not try to stop content. If a brief content-light interval occurs, do nothing to extend it.

### Cue D1-H-1440 — 24:00 — Coach — Pure Observation

> Ask once, silently: what is aware of this experience? Do not answer in words. Remain alert. If you are falling asleep, deepen one breath and feel the chair.

### Cue D1-H-1480 — 24:40 — Required return

> Take one slightly deeper breath. Feel the chair and both feet. Move the fingers gently. Open the eyes. Name the room, the time of day, and what you will do next. Keep a small amount of broad awareness as you return.

### End — 25:00

The session ends at exactly 1,500 seconds. Completion is not granted until local persistence succeeds or is safely queued.

## 8. Post-session voice-first debrief

The first screen after practice asks:

> Speak what happened before interpreting it.

Suggested raw-capture prompts:

- What physical changes occurred?
- Could you retain appreciation after releasing the memory?
- How quickly did you recognize attention capture?
- Did awareness feel broader than ordinary focus?
- Did any content-light interval occur with continuous awareness?
- Were you alert, drowsy, strained, anxious, or comfortable?

Interpretation appears only after the raw recording is saved.

## 9. Capability-credit rule

### Morning completion

Requires:

- natural 1,500-second completion;
- no required-audio failure or unauthorized silent continuation;
- safe return;
- local completion record saved or queued.

### Day 1 state accessed

Requires at least two of:

- physical effort clearly reduced;
- appreciation retained for at least 30 seconds without replaying the scene;
- distraction recognized and attention deliberately returned;
- open monitoring held clearly for at least two minutes;
- remembered content-light interval with continuous awareness.

And:

- alertness score at least 2;
- no unresolved safety symptom.

A complete timer without markers earns practice completion, not state-access credit.
