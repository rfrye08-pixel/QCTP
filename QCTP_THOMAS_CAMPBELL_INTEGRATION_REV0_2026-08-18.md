## 1. Purpose

Integrate Thomas Campbell’s publicly described work into QCTP as a major source track connecting:

- meditation and point consciousness;
- open-minded skepticism;
- intent;
- fear, ego, and belief-trap reduction;
- imaginality;
- remote information gathering;
- probable-future experiments;
- OBE/nonphysical exploration;
- healing-intent experiments;
- daily quality-of-consciousness development.

Campbell’s track is valuable because it links nonordinary-state practice with experimental discipline and everyday choice quality.

## 2. Source lineage

Campbell’s public biography and Monroe Institute material describe his early work with Robert Monroe and Dennis Mennerich on consciousness exploration and audio-assisted states. QCTP shall treat Campbell as related to the Monroe lineage but not identical to Monroe’s Focus-level system.

Public source set:

- My Big TOE official site: `https://www.my-big-toe.com/`
- Exploring Consciousness and Everything Paranormal: `https://www.my-big-toe.com/shop/exploring-consciousness-everything-paranormal/`
- Binaural Beats: `https://www.my-big-toe.com/shop/binaural-beats/`
- Tom’s Park / Imaginality training: `https://www.my-big-toe.com/shop/toms-park-virtual-imaginality-training-program/`
- Monroe Institute conversation with Thomas Campbell: `https://www.monroeinstitute.org/blogs/podcasts/research-and-out-of-body-experiences-with-robert-a-monroe-a-conversation-with-tom-campbell-physicist-and-author`

QCTP shall not reproduce paid audio instructions, book passages, proprietary exercises, or course scripts. It shall create original exercises from publicly described principles.

## 3. Controlled terminology

### 3.1 My Big TOE model

A source-specific conceptual model in which consciousness and information are fundamental and physical reality is treated as a virtual-reality data stream.

QCTP presents this as Campbell’s model. It is not silently adopted as the only QCTP ontology.

### 3.2 Point consciousness

A stable meditation platform in which ordinary physical sensory data and intellectual chatter become less dominant while awareness remains continuous.

QCTP relationship:

- possible phenomenological overlap with Gap and sensory-withdrawal states;
- possible prerequisite analogue to Monroe Focus work;
- not automatically identical to Focus 10, Focus 15, or any “quantum field” label.

### 3.3 Data stream

Campbell’s model term for an organized information flow experienced as a reality frame or experiential environment.

QCTP use:

- source-specific explanatory term;
- not a generic replacement for imagination, perception, dream, or sensory input;
- every session still distinguishes deliberate construction, spontaneous content, and verified feedback.

### 3.4 Intent

The underlying direction and quality of consciousness expressed through choice, not merely a verbal desire or visualization.

QCTP use:

- compare stated intention, emotional reaction, behavior, and outcome;
- separate fear/control/ego motive from care, clarity, and responsibility;
- use Mirror and daily integration records.

### 3.5 Quality of consciousness

Campbell’s practical transformation target involving less fear, ego, and rigid belief and more care, authenticity, responsibility, cooperation, and love.

QCTP use:

- behavior and relationship integration;
- no claim of advancement from altered-state intensity alone.

### 3.6 Entropy reduction

Campbell’s source-specific information-system metaphor for increasing organization and quality of consciousness.

QCTP does not convert this into a literal thermodynamic measurement of a person.

### 3.7 Belief trap

A belief held rigidly enough to constrain perception, interpretation, or exploration.

QCTP applies the term symmetrically to belief and disbelief.

### 3.8 Open-minded skepticism

A disciplined posture of considering possibilities, demanding personal data and feedback, updating confidence, and avoiding premature certainty.

This becomes a core Lab method.

### 3.9 Imaginality

Campbell’s term associated with developing a stable intuitive inner environment, creative capacity, problem solving, and experience beyond ordinary physical focus.

QCTP creates original Stable Imaginality Environment exercises rather than copying Tom’s Park.

## 4. Track architecture

The Thomas Campbell track contains ten controlled modules. It runs parallel to the Foundation Path and advances by completion and capability gates rather than calendar dates.

## TC-01 — Open-Minded Skepticism

### Learning objectives

- distinguish possibility, probability, belief, disbelief, and personal knowledge;
- recognize confirmation-seeking;
- preserve contrary evidence;
- update confidence without swinging between certainty and dismissal.

### Original QCTP exercise — Possibility Ledger

For one claim or practice, record:

1. claim in neutral language;
2. what would count as supporting data;
3. what would count as contrary data;
4. current confidence from 0–100%;
5. one blinded or feedback-bearing experiment;
6. result;
7. updated confidence;
8. what remains unresolved.

### Completion gate

Complete three ledgers in which confidence changes in response to data at least once.

## TC-02 — Meditation Mechanics and Point Consciousness

### Learning objectives

- reduce physical sensory engagement without strain;
- reduce intellectual narration;
- maintain alert continuity;
- hold one nonverbal intent;
- distinguish point consciousness from forced darkness, sleep, and dissociation.

### Practice

Use `TC-PC — Point Consciousness` from the State Atlas.

### Completion gate

Stabilize the state according to the State Atlas before using it for information-gathering experiments.

## TC-03 — Intellect, Intuition, and Noise

### Learning objectives

- recognize first-pass impressions;
- identify intellectual elaboration;
- identify fear and desire contamination;
- use feedback to calibrate intuition.

### Original QCTP drill — First / Second / Story

For each prompt:

- `FIRST` — record the first brief sensation, word, image, or directional pull within three seconds;
- `SECOND` — record the next impression;
- `STORY` — record the explanation the intellect constructs;
- obtain feedback where possible;
- compare which layer carried useful information.

### Completion gate

Complete twenty feedback-bearing micro-trials and review confidence calibration.

## TC-04 — Intent Quality

### Learning objectives

- distinguish stated desire from underlying intent;
- identify control, approval, fear, status, and avoidance motives;
- define a more caring and responsible action without becoming passive.

### Original QCTP exercise — Intent Stack

For one real choice, record:

1. What do I say I want?
2. What outcome am I trying to control?
3. What am I afraid will happen?
4. What do I need another person to think about me?
5. What action protects my image?
6. What action best expresses care, truth, courage, and responsibility?
7. What did I actually do?
8. What outcome followed?

### Completion gate

Complete five real-life Intent Stacks with later outcome review.

## TC-05 — Fear, Ego, and Belief Audit

### Learning objectives

- recognize recurring triggers;
- identify identity protection;
- identify beliefs that turn uncertainty into threat;
- practice lower-fear choices.

### Original QCTP protocol — Trigger Reconstruction

Record:

- observable event;
- immediate body reaction;
- feared outcome;
- self-image under threat;
- judgment of the other person;
- belief that made the event threatening;
- alternative interpretation;
- lower-fear action;
- later outcome.

### Mirror integration

Mirror Core and Local AI Mirror may identify repeated triggers and intent/action mismatches, but every conclusion must cite exact records and remain revisable.

### Completion gate

Identify one repeated trigger across at least three events and perform one lower-fear behavioral experiment.

## TC-06 — Stable Imaginality

### Learning objectives

- build a stable repeatable inner environment;
- improve multisensory construction;
- distinguish deliberate construction from spontaneous development;
- use the environment for problem solving without treating every event as external information.

### Practice

Use `QI — Stable Imaginality Environment` from the State Atlas.

### Completion gate

Re-enter and map the same environment across three sessions with stable anchor relationships.

## TC-07 — Remote Information Experiments

### Learning objectives

- use point consciousness or receiver state;
- work from blind target IDs;
- preserve raw descriptors;
- separate analytical overlay;
- score feedback honestly.

### Experiment series

1. concealed object target;
2. concealed photograph target;
3. known-location detail target;
4. past-event target with documented feedback;
5. near-future target selected by later randomization.

### Controls

- protocol locked before capture;
- no target clues;
- raw data immutable after feedback;
- correspondence and confidence scored separately;
- misses retained.

### Completion gate

Complete a ten-session blinded set before drawing conclusions.

## TC-08 — Probable Future and Decision Trees

### Learning objectives

- treat future impressions as probabilities rather than destiny;
- separate prediction from preference and fear;
- use multiple branches;
- obtain feedback at the defined future date.

### Original QCTP protocol — Branch Forecast

For one decision:

- define option A and option B;
- define a specific future feedback date;
- enter receiver state;
- record first-pass impressions for A;
- reset state;
- record first-pass impressions for B;
- list known practical factors separately;
- make the actual decision without treating the session as infallible;
- return on the feedback date;
- score correspondence and decision usefulness.

### Completion gate

Complete five forecast cycles with delayed feedback.

## TC-09 — OBE, Data-Stream Transition, and Healing Intent

### Learning objectives

- use Focus 10 or point consciousness as a stable platform;
- attempt transition without breath holding or physical strain;
- record continuity and verification;
- perform healing-intent work under controlled ethical boundaries.

### OBE practice

Use `QO — OBE Threshold and Separation Practice` from the State Atlas.

### Healing-intent experiment boundaries

- no diagnosis;
- no replacement of medical care;
- consent where another person is involved;
- define target, duration, and outcome measure before practice;
- record no-effect and contrary outcomes;
- separate compassionate intent from certainty claims.

### Completion gate

OBE: coherent continuity record and verification attempt.  
Healing intent: complete five predefined sessions with outcome review.

## TC-10 — Quality of Consciousness in Daily Life

### Learning objectives

- translate altered-state work into choices;
- reduce fear-driven behavior;
- improve relationship quality;
- increase authenticity and responsibility;
- make care operational.

### Weekly integration loop

1. select one repeated fear or control pattern;
2. select one relationship context;
3. define one lower-fear behavior;
4. perform it;
5. record outcome;
6. review through Mirror;
7. revise.

### Completion artifact

Create a Personal Theory of Practice containing:

- current model of consciousness;
- confidence levels;
- direct experiences;
- unresolved questions;
- reliable practices;
- known failure modes;
- ethical boundaries;
- daily-life transformation priorities.

## 7. Daily and weekly cadence

### Daily micro-layer

One optional prompt:

- What fear is active?
- What am I trying to control?
- What is my first impression before analysis?
- What belief am I treating as fact?
- What lower-fear action is available?

### Weekly practice layer

One 25–45-minute Campbell practice or experiment after prerequisites are met.

### Monthly synthesis

Review:

- point-consciousness reliability;
- experiment calibration;
- recurring fear and intent patterns;
- behavior changes;
- current model confidence;
- unresolved questions.

## 8. Evidence and record discipline

Each Campbell-derived record must preserve:

- source concept;
- QCTP practice ID;
- raw experience;
- interpretation;
- confidence;
- feedback;
- later revision;
- daily-life implication.

The app shall not use Campbell’s model to automatically explain every dream, OBE, intuition, synchronicity, or geometry experience.

## 9. Safety and grounding

- no altered-state practice while driving, using machinery, on ladders, in water, or in hazardous contexts;
- no prolonged retention or hyperventilation in Campbell point-consciousness work;
- stop and orient if fear, panic, disorientation, or persistent derealization occurs;
- maintain sleep and daily functioning;
- healing-intent work does not replace professional care;
- future impressions do not remove practical responsibility or informed decision-making.

## 10. Intellectual-property controls

- link to public source material;
- summarize concepts in original language;
- do not copy paid audio scripts, books, exercises, or course worksheets;
- label QCTP original practices;
- preserve Campbell-specific terminology and attribution;
- store user-created records separately from source content.
