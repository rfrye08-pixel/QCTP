## 4.2 `HM-QC-01` — HeartMath Quick Coherence® Technique

**Primary official reference:** `https://www.heartmath.com/resources/quick-coherance-technique/`  
**Evidence class:** `PUBLISHED OFFICIAL METHOD`  
**Day 1 role:** exact coherence and appreciation baseline.

### Source method

1. Focus attention in the area of the heart.
2. Imagine the breath flowing in and out through the heart or chest area.
3. Breathe a little slower and deeper than usual.
4. HeartMath suggests five seconds in and five seconds out, or another comfortable rhythm.
5. Make a sincere attempt to experience a regenerative feeling such as appreciation or care; calm or ease may be used when appreciation is difficult.

### Source limits that QCTP must preserve

- The five/five rhythm is a suggestion with a comfort override, not an inflexible pass/fail requirement.
- The official technique does not require a breath hold.
- The official page does not specify nasal-only breathing.
- The emotional step is an attempted felt experience, not merely repeating a positive word.

### QCTP enhancement boundary

QCTP may count the first few cycles, provide a quiet breath rail, add volume safeguards, and explain how to reduce effort. It may not replace the source's five/five-or-comfortable baseline with four-in/six-out and still label the result HeartMath-derived.

## 4.3 `JD-SPACE-01` — Dr. Joe Dispenza spatial-attention induction

**Primary official references:**

- `https://drjoedispenza.com/dr-joes-blog/the-role-of-brainwaves-in-meditation-part-i`
- `https://support.drjoedispenza.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022266072-Product-What-does-Dr-Joe-mean-when-he-refers-to-space`
- `https://support.drjoedispenza.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019793837-What-is-the-induction-to-a-meditation`

**Evidence class:** `PUBLISHED OFFICIAL METHOD DESCRIPTION`  
**Day 1 role:** introduce the operational difference between narrow/object attention and broad spatial attention.

### Source method

1. Close the eyes and reduce engagement with incoming sensory information.
2. Recognize narrow or object-focused attention.
3. Open and broaden attention toward space or nothingness.
4. Sense or feel the space around the body rather than merely thinking about the word `space`.
5. A source example is awareness of the space between the ears while retaining a sense of that area in space.
6. The induction commonly moves attention to a body area and then to the space around that area.

### Day 1 scope limit

Day 1 uses this only as an introductory attention-control exercise. It does not claim that Ryan reached alpha, theta, `no body/no one/no thing`, the quantum field, or any other advanced source-stated outcome. Those are later-state questions and require direct experience and progression gates.

### QCTP enhancement boundary

QCTP may:

- divide the progression into head, whole-body, and room-scale steps;
- coach the distinction between sensing space and constructing a visual scene;
- add predictable cue entry and a continuous support bed;
- prompt attention recovery when the mind returns to an object or story.

It may not replace the spatial-attention operation with generic open-monitoring language and continue to attribute it to Dispenza.
