## 1. Purpose

Integrate Robert Edward Grant's methods into QCTP as a practical, hands-on consciousness and polymathic-development track.

This is not a collection of quotes and it is not a replacement for the 112-day Foundation Path. It is a structured secondary path that adds geometry, number, sound, symbol, creative synthesis, mirror work, and applied daily precepts.

## 2. Primary source map

### 2.1 Philosophical Geometry

A free, hands-on course built around geometry drawing and philosophy. Public topics include drawing geometry, the cuboctahedron, nested geometries, perspective polytopes, the Granthahedron, the vesica piscis and Flower of Life, the hexapentakis, the Mirror of Consciousness, Vitruvian Man, the eye, mirror reflections, wave-number theory, octagonal perspective, and the relationship between sound and light.

Official source: `https://robertedwardgrant.com/courses/Philosophical-Geometry/`

### 2.2 Mindfulness & Meditative Geometry

A ten-class series using geometry construction as a mindfulness practice.

Official source: `https://robertedwardgrant.com/courses/mindfulness-meditative-geometry-series/`

### 2.3 The Etymology of Number

A four-part lecture sequence connecting number to physics, chemistry, photonics, gravity, music, art, architecture, measurement, time, awareness, mathematical constants, and the Mirror of Consciousness.

Official source: `https://robertedwardgrant.com/courses/the-etymology-of-number/`

### 2.4 The Language of Light

An advanced course connecting mathematics, prime patterns, music, art, ancient symbols, geometry, consciousness, and practical creative problem-solving. The source description explicitly emphasizes applied learning and auto-dictation.

Official source: `https://robertedwardgrant.com/courses/the-language-of-light/`

### 2.5 24 Precepts of Universal Mind

A daily-life framework emphasizing self-observation, nonjudgment, shadow integration, gratitude, self-care, the Quadrivium, kindness/service, synchronicity, emotional-state modulation, intentionality, vulnerability, creativity, and teachability.

Official source: `https://robertedwardgrant.com/24-precepts-of-universal-mind/`

### 2.6 Architect+ / mirror-intelligence concept

A contemporary product concept framing AI as a reflective companion for self-development, awareness expansion, symbolic inquiry, and deeper questioning.

QCTP will not copy Architect+. The relevant transferable product primitive is a longitudinal AI mirror that reflects the user's own patterns while preserving raw records and user authority.

## 5. Delivery schedule inside QCTP

### Daily micro-layer

A two-to-five-minute prompt may be attached to the Foundation day:

- today's precept;
- mirror question;
- pattern to observe;
- one integration action.

### Studio layer

Two sessions per seven completed Foundation days:

- 30–45 minutes;
- eyes open;
- seated at a desk or work surface;
- geometry drawing, number exploration, or harmonic work;
- not combined with driving, machinery, shop cutting, ladders, or other hazardous activity.

### Deep synthesis layer

One 45–75 minute session per completed Foundation week:

- geometry construction;
- source lesson or visual explanation;
- auto-dictation;
- journal and artifact capture.

The app advances these sessions by completion, not calendar date.

## 7. First original QCTP Grant session

### REG-01-A — Learn to See

**Duration:** 35 minutes  
**Mode:** eyes-open studio  
**Materials:** compass, straightedge, pencil, paper, journal/app.

#### Phase 1 — State entry: 5 minutes

- sit upright;
- three coherence breaths;
- soften gaze;
- observe the blank page as a field rather than an empty object;
- notice anticipation, impatience, and the urge to rush.

#### Phase 2 — Construction: 15 minutes

1. mark a center point;
2. set one compass radius and do not change it;
3. draw one circle slowly;
4. draw horizontal and vertical diameters through the center;
5. place the compass point on the circle boundary and draw a second equal circle through the original center;
6. identify the overlap, centers, intersections, and shared chord;
7. repeat once on a second sheet with improved precision.

#### Phase 3 — Observation without interpretation: 5 minutes

Record only visible or measurable facts:

- line quality;
- symmetry;
- spacing;
- intersection locations;
- construction error;
- body tension;
- attention changes.

#### Phase 4 — Auto-dictation: 5 minutes

Write continuously from the prompt:

`What did the act of constructing reveal that looking at a finished image would not have revealed?`

Do not edit or organize.

#### Phase 5 — Integration: 5 minutes

- underline one sentence from the auto-dictation;
- choose one daily-life application;
- photograph the construction;
- save the raw image and reflection as separate records.
