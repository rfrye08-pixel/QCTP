# QCTP Source Fidelity and Guidance Enhancement Standard — Rev0

**Document ID:** `QCTP-SOURCE-FIDELITY-REV0`  
**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Status:** `CONTROLLED CURRICULUM AND MEDITATION AUTHORITY`  
**Release authority:** `ZERO_RELEASE`  
**User correction incorporated:** The guidance taught by the people and source traditions in the QCTP course is the baseline. QCTP improves that guidance; it does not replace it with an independently invented meditation.

## 1. Purpose

QCTP is a source-grounded training platform. Its Foundation Path and source tracks exist to teach, integrate, and improve the actual methods described by the selected teachers and practitioner lineages.

The controlled production model is:

`PRIMARY SOURCE METHOD -> SOURCE-FAITHFUL BASELINE -> QCTP ENHANCEMENT DELTA -> AUDIO/APP IMPLEMENTATION -> VERIFICATION -> USER ACCEPTANCE`

The source method controls the practice objective, sequence, attentional operation, imagery, terminology, breath method, transition logic, and intended state. QCTP may make that method clearer, safer, more executable, more reliable, and more usable on an iPhone, but shall not silently substitute a different method.

## 2. Authority order

For every source-derived lesson or meditation, precedence is:

1. Ryan's explicit course objective and approved source set;
2. the applicable teacher's or lineage's primary public guidance;
3. the controlled source-practice crosswalk for that lesson;
4. the QCTP enhancement delta;
5. audio production, app behavior, and presentation;
6. general meditation conventions.

General QCTP reasoning may not override a source method merely because another cadence, metaphor, state map, or audio profile appears technically preferable.

## 3. Practice classes

Every practice must be labeled as one of four classes.

### 3.1 `SOURCE_FAITHFUL`

An original QCTP paraphrase that preserves one source's method, order, state target, and terminology without copying protected script text.

Permitted changes are limited to clarity, pacing, accessibility, safety, and implementation reliability.

### 3.2 `SOURCE_ENHANCED`

The default class for the integrated Foundation Path.

It contains:

- one identified source method as the baseline;
- an explicit source-faithful core;
- separately identified QCTP enhancements;
- no silent change to the source's actual technique.

### 3.3 `QCTP_SYNTHESIS`

A controlled integration of two or more source methods. Every segment must identify its source, and every transition between methods must be explicit.

A synthesis may be used only when the methods are compatible for the target state and the combined sequence is justified in the source crosswalk.

### 3.4 `QCTP_ORIGINAL`

An original QCTP exercise that is not presented as the method of a named teacher or lineage. It may draw on general principles, but must remain clearly labeled.

## 4. Required source record

Before a source-derived script is locked, its source record must contain:

- teacher, organization, or practitioner lineage;
- specific work, video, interview, book, public course page, protocol, or demonstration;
- exact public reference and, where available, timestamps or page/section locations;
- whether the source is public, purchased, proprietary, or unavailable;
- source terminology;
- target state or capability;
- prerequisite state;
- posture and eye condition;
- breath route, cadence, volume, holds, duration, and release instruction when specified;
- physical settling or preparatory sequence;
- attentional object or field;
- imagery perspective and construction/reception distinction;
- emotional or intentional operation;
- induction sequence;
- stabilization method;
- task or use phase;
- state markers;
- corrections and stop conditions;
- return sequence;
- source use of music, binaural audio, tones, silence, counting, verbal cues, or haptics;
- unresolved or ambiguous instructions;
- intellectual-property constraints.

No meditation may be called source-faithful while this record is absent.

## 5. Source-practice crosswalk

Every lesson or meditation must contain a segment-level crosswalk:

| Segment | Source | Source operation | QCTP wording | QCTP enhancement | Fidelity status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Allowed fidelity statuses:

- `DIRECTLY SOURCE-DERIVED`
- `SOURCE-COMPATIBLE CLARIFICATION`
- `QCTP SUPPORT LAYER`
- `QCTP SYNTHESIS TRANSITION`
- `UNRESOLVED — DO NOT RENDER`

The crosswalk is the content authority used by script review, audio production, testing, and later revision.

## 6. Allowed QCTP enhancements

QCTP may improve a source practice by adding or correcting:

- physically executable wording;
- timing and deliberate pauses;
- slower, more conversational narration;
- predictable pre-cue markers and voice fades;
- a continuous sound bed that prevents abrupt voice entry;
- local/offline audio packaging;
- volume normalization and transient control;
- accessibility text and controls;
- exact safety and stop conditions;
- troubleshooting for ambiguity, over-effort, drowsiness, discomfort, or distraction;
- readiness checks;
- pause/resume and fail-closed behavior;
- voice-first debrief;
- raw-observation preservation;
- state-marker and capability tracking;
- bounded adaptation from Ryan's reports.

These are enhancement layers. They do not authorize changing the practice itself.

## 7. Prohibited silent substitutions

Without an explicit `QCTP_SYNTHESIS` or `QCTP_ORIGINAL` designation, QCTP shall not:

- replace the source's breath method with QCTP-B1, box breathing, or another preferred cadence;
- reorder the source's induction sequence;
- replace the source's attentional object;
- change deliberate imagery into receptive observation or vice versa;
- substitute one teacher's terminology for another's;
- collapse Monroe Focus states, Campbell point consciousness, QCTP Gap, Dispenza field language, HeartMath coherence, energy-center work, and other systems into one unnamed state;
- add a state claim or expected sensation not present in the source;
- attribute a QCTP support feature to the teacher;
- remove a source component because it is unconventional or difficult to explain;
- combine methods merely to fill a timetable.

## 8. Breath rule

Breathing follows this hierarchy:

1. When the source specifies a breath method, preserve it as the baseline.
2. When the source describes breathing qualitatively but incompletely, reconstruct the most faithful operational version from that source's broader public method.
3. When important details remain absent, add an optional `QCTP SUPPORT` clarification only if it does not change the intended practice.
4. When the source does not use deliberate breath control, any QCTP regulation phase must occur before the source practice, be labeled separately, and be released before the source induction begins.
5. QCTP-B1 is not a universal replacement for source-specific breathing.

## 9. Sound and binaural rule

- When sustained sound, binaural beats, Hemi-Sync/Monroe Sound Science, or another audio technology is integral to the source method, preserve that role and source attribution.
- When the source does not require binaural audio, QCTP may offer an optional support bed, clearly labeled `QCTP SUPPORT LAYER`.
- A support bed may improve continuity and reduce startle, but may not be represented as part of a teacher's method unless the source establishes it.
- Frequency trajectories, carrier levels, and ambient textures remain engineering candidates until physical acceptance.

## 10. Improvement rule

QCTP improves the baseline by asking:

1. What exactly does the source tell the practitioner to do?
2. What is the intended state or capability?
3. Which instructions are implicit, vague, poorly timed, or difficult to execute?
4. Which delivery defects interrupt the practice?
5. What can be clarified without changing the method?
6. What app/audio support can make the method easier to perform correctly?
7. How will the result be verified against both source fidelity and Ryan's direct experience?

The objective is not a generic better meditation. It is a better implementation of the selected source method.

## 11. Intellectual-property rule

QCTP shall not reproduce paid meditation scripts, paid audio, proprietary worksheets, or substantial protected passages.

It may:

- study authorized or publicly described methods;
- record source facts and sequence;
- write original source-faithful guidance;
- link to primary material;
- distinguish public-source extraction from QCTP enhancement;
- preserve Ryan's own notes and experiences.

When the only available authority is inaccessible proprietary content, the segment is marked `UNRESOLVED — DO NOT RENDER` rather than invented.

## 13. Acceptance criteria

A source-derived QCTP meditation may advance to voice rendering only when:

1. the primary source set is identified;
2. the source record is complete;
3. every segment has a crosswalk entry;
4. unresolved segments are closed or removed;
5. the source-faithful baseline is reviewed;
6. every QCTP enhancement is separately labeled;
7. no protected script is copied;
8. source terminology and state distinctions are preserved;
9. the enhancement layer passes content and safety review;
10. release authority remains explicit.

## 14. Release status

This standard is immediately controlling for all future QCTP lessons, meditations, source tracks, and guidance revisions. It changes process and content authority, not the currently installed runtime.
