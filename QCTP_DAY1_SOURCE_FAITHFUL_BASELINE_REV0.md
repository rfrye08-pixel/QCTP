## 2. Method classification

Day 1 is a `QCTP_SYNTHESIS` composed of:

- Bullard-derived contraction awareness;
- HeartMath Quick Coherence;
- Dispenza-derived body-area-to-space induction;
- QCTP baseline observation, transitions, pure observation, return, and recording.

The teachers' methods remain distinct. The transition between methods is operationally explicit.

## 3. Source-faithful 25-minute baseline

### 00:00–00:45 — QCTP baseline observation

Sit with the feet supported and the hands resting. Close the eyes. Before changing the breath, posture, emotion, or width of attention, notice the current mental tone, strongest emotion, natural breathing, and the effort present in the jaw, shoulders, chest, and abdomen.

### 00:45–03:00 — Bullard-derived contraction awareness

Locate one clear area of contraction in the gut, chest, muscles, or general bracing. Feel the physical contraction without suppressing it and without searching for a story. If the body is ready, allow some of the holding to release. If nothing changes, remain accurate and accepting. Invite a constructive quality such as acceptance, gratitude, peace, or love into any space that opens.

### 03:00–08:00 — HeartMath Quick Coherence

Place attention in the heart area. Imagine that the breath moves in and out through the heart or chest. Breathe slightly slower and deeper than usual. Use approximately five seconds in and five seconds out, or another rhythm that remains comfortable. Make a sincere attempt to experience appreciation or care. A person, pet, place, accomplishment, or meaningful moment may help evoke the feeling. Calm or ease may be used when appreciation is not available. Continue heart-focused breathing while attending to the genuine feeling.

### 08:00–13:00 — Dispenza-derived body-area-to-space induction

Let deliberate breath timing end. Bring awareness to the region between the ears and sense the volume it occupies. Then include the space immediately around the head. Notice the difference between attention aimed at a body area and attention opened to the space around that area. Continue from the head to the chest, abdomen, and whole body as volumes existing in space.

### 13:00–23:00 — Dispenza-derived open spatial awareness

Sense the space surrounding the whole body: in front, behind, above, below, and on both sides. Expand to the room as one volume. Include sounds as events occurring within the same field rather than following one sound. When attention becomes captured, return to one body-area-and-space reference, then reopen to the whole room. Continue sensing rather than constructing a visual scene.

### 23:00–24:00 — QCTP pure observation

Stop deliberately widening attention. Release heart-focused breathing, emotional generation, and spatial induction. Observe the current mind, body, emotion, breath, and sense of space without grading the experience.

### 24:00–25:00 — QCTP return

Feel the feet, chair, and hands. Let the breath be natural. Re-establish the room, time of day, and next intended action. Move gently and open the eyes. Select one quality from the practice to carry into ordinary activity.

- OBE, remote viewing, or presentiment;
- that a selected audio frequency causes the intended state.

Those outcomes require separate source tracks and direct evidence.
