## 3. Audio and anti-startle improvements

1. Use one continuous voice stem and one continuous support stem, with a pre-rendered composite fallback.
2. Maintain a low-information ambient bed throughout the timed session; no voice starts from digital silence.
3. Before every non-opening cue, duck the bed, play the same soft marker, and fade the voice in and out.
4. Package all media locally and fail closed if the media clock, files, or synchronization are not proven.
5. Keep narration within controlled Teach-tier rates; use one executable action per sentence where practical.

These features are `QCTP SUPPORT LAYER` elements and shall not be attributed to Bullard, HeartMath, or Dispenza.

## 4. Binaural support disposition

Binaural audio remains available as an optional QCTP support mode because Ryan requested it and because continuous stereo sound may improve continuity. It is not part of the selected Day 1 source methods.

A02 does **not** lock a difference-frequency trajectory. The app must support:

- `AMBIENT` — continuous bed without binaural difference;
- `BINAURAL LOW` — candidate stereo difference tested at low level;
- `MINIMAL CONTINUITY` — voice plus marker over a barely audible continuity floor; no return to digital silence.

The exact binaural profile must be selected by A/B testing for comfort, startle reduction, alertness, and state markers. No capability credit may be assigned from the frequency setting alone.

## 5. Breath-coaching improvements

- Count one five-second inhale and one five-second exhale in real time.
- Continue with a quiet rail rather than repeated verbal counting.
- State that five/five is a suggestion and comfort controls.
- Do not add a hold.
- Do not require nasal-only breathing because the source does not specify it.
- End deliberate pacing before the Dispenza induction.

## 6. Guidance-density improvements

Teach tier uses more frequent cues early, then progressively less speech:

- baseline and Bullard segment: approximately 20–32 seconds between cues;
- HeartMath teaching: approximately 30–50 seconds between cues;
- initial spatial induction: approximately 45–65 seconds between cues;
- stabilized broad space: approximately 65–100 seconds between cues, over a continuous bed and predictable marker;
- return: multiple short cues within the final minute.

Long intervals remain available for practice, but they are no longer perceptually unannounced.

## 7. Adaptation controls

After the session, QCTP may alter only bounded delivery parameters unless a later controlled content action changes the source method:

- voice/bed balance;
- marker level;
- guidance tier;
- breath-rail level;
- ambient versus binaural support;
- optional correction prompts.

It may not silently change the HeartMath cadence, Bullard operation, Dispenza attention target, source order, or phase boundaries.
